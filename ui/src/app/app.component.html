About this system

Pick a state and office above to see a facility's current recommended posture, who it would notify, and why — built to evaluate any operational signal relevant to a facility, not only weather. Weather is the primary source today; the same evaluation and notification pipeline is designed to take on additional signals as they come online.

Data sources currently connected

  • National Weather Service (NWS) — active alerts, used for every facility.
  • USGS / NOAA river gauges — flood category and stage, for facilities near a monitored river.
  • AQICN air quality — current AQI, for facilities within range of a monitoring station.
  • Iowa DOT road conditions — winter road-condition reports, for Iowa facilities.

Each facility can also have its own curated reference links (local news, county emergency management, etc.) — manage those from a facility's page once selected.
